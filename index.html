Where do these numbers come from?
  • Writ rep: Journeyman 75, Expert 125, Artisan 200 per delivery.
  • Daily limit: each named order counts once a day. There are about 45 Journeyman, 60 Expert and 45 Artisan orders. Different orders share no cap, and extra copies keep for later days.
  • Levels: Neutral 3,000 · Friendly 6,000 · Honored 12,000 · Revered 21,000 (42,000 from Neutral to Exalted).
  • Shipping Label: one-time quest from your first crate or writ, +2,000 rep.
  • Forged Delivery Receipt: 15 Merchant's Favor, needs Friendly, turns an empty Waylaid Crate into 50 rep.
  • Diplomat: +2% rep per rank. This comes from a single guide and hasn't been confirmed.
  • Sources: community guides from November 2026 (wowforever.be, conquestcapped.com, wow-professions.com). WoW Forever is new, so hotfixes may change these numbers.
